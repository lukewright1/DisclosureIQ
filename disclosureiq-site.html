The problem

Disclosure delivery is broken
for most advisory firms.

📧
Emails get ignored — and you can't prove delivery
Sending ADV brochures as email attachments leaves no audit trail. If the SEC asks for proof of delivery, you have nothing.
📋
Manual tracking in spreadsheets doesn't scale
Tracking who received what, when, and whether they acknowledged it — manually — breaks down fast as your client base grows.
🔄
SEC IAPD updates slip through the cracks
When your ADV is updated on IAPD, there's no automatic trigger to redistribute it. Clients end up with stale versions — a real exam risk.
✉️
Physical mail is a manual nightmare
Clients who need paper copies require printing, stuffing, and mailing — then tracking. Most firms either skip it or can't prove they did it.
DisclosureIQ solves this

One platform. Every delivery. Every proof.

✓ Secure client portal with timestamped acknowledgement gate — clients sign before they can even view docs
✓ Automatic SEC IAPD sync — get alerted when your filed version changes and redistribution triggers automatically
✓ Physical mail via Lob API — print, stuff, and USPS-track letters without touching a printer
✓ Immutable audit trail — every delivery, view, signature, and mail event is logged with timestamp and IP
✓ Redtail & Orion integration — sync clients automatically, no manual imports
✓ Per-document delivery scheduling — set annual, quarterly, or event-triggered sends per document type
