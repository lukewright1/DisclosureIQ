How it works
1
You hit Send in DisclosureIQ — recipients, documents, and message are all selected.
2
DisclosureIQ calls your Power Automate flow with each client's name, email, subject, and personalised message body.
3
Power Automate sends the email from your real Outlook address — no third party involved.
4
Every send is logged in DisclosureIQ's delivery log with timestamp and status.
5
Power Automate also logs every run independently — giving you a dual audit trail within Microsoft 365.
